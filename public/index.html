Brazilian tax regimes

For this estimate, service amounts are the payout you want to receive. The selected rate is grossed up so the invoice total is higher and the target payout is preserved. This is not a determination of your actual tax or withholding obligations; confirm those with an accountant.

Simples Nacional 6%

This app uses an illustrative 6% rate for this regime. The actual rate depends on the business and its circumstances.

In this estimate: the app divides the target payout by 0.94 to calculate the invoice total. For a R$ 10,000.00 target payout, that is R$ 10,638.30 total, including a R$ 638.30 gross-up adjustment.

MEI 0%

Short for Microempreendedor Individual — individual micro-entrepreneur, the lightest business category in Brazil, aimed at self-employed professionals. A MEI settles taxes through DAS, a fixed monthly contribution that does not vary with revenue.

In this estimate: the rate is 0%, so the invoice total equals the target payout. This does not represent or calculate the MEI's separate tax obligations.
